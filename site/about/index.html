About this suite

Vibecoded, and for personal use first.

Read this before you install anything. It says plainly how the apps on this site are made, what they are for, and what that means if you want to use one.

In one paragraph

Every app on this site is vibecoded: Turbo tells an AI (Claude) what is wanted, looks at what comes back, and steers until it is right. The code was not written by hand, and it is not presented as hand-crafted work. What comes from a person is the vision: what each app is for, how it should feel, what it must never do, and every decision about it.

How it works

What “vibecoded” means here

A person describes an app in plain words, an AI writes it, and the person runs it, reads what came back, and says what is wrong. Round after round, until it does what was wanted. The person decides; the AI types.

That is a different thing from software written by hand, and it should not be mistaken for it. These apps are not hand-crafted, and nothing on this site says they are.

  • From a person: what each app is for, the way it should feel, what it must never do, and every decision about how it behaves.
  • From the AI: the code, and the tests that go with it.
  • Checked by running it: where an app’s own notes say something has not been run, such as on a phone, this site says so too, and calls the app early.
For personal use

Made to fit one life first

The apps exist because they were wanted for everyday life, so they fit those habits before anyone else’s. If they suit you too, great. Expect rough edges, and expect them to change when those needs do.

They are shared because they are open source and might be useful, not because they are products built for everyone. Read an app’s README before you rely on it, and keep your own backups of anything you cannot lose.

The pictures

About the screenshots

Every screenshot is a real capture of the running app, taken with a script in a real browser. Nothing on this site is drawn, mocked up or made by an image model to look like an app.

  • The data is invented. The artists, recipes, notes, budget and videos are made up for the captures.
  • The pictures inside the apps are drawn by a script. The album covers, recipe pictures and the clip in TurboCut are simple drawings made with code, standing in for the real photos a real library would have. They are not photographs.
  • Each capture is made twice, in a dark and a light version, and the page shows the one that matches the look you have chosen.
  • Two apps have no screenshots. TurboBox is an Android app and TurboMC Server needs a Qt build, and neither could be run where the captures were made.
